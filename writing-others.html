Others

Sometimes I help others write or edit writing for others, in the context of college applications, but also elsewhere. Here are some pieces I've (signficantly) helped with. I endorse some (but not all) of the prose and some (but not all) of the ideas below:

  • Joint Statement about Mathathon, a statement by Mathathon organizers and others on reorganizing their event; it is cross-posted also to Terence Tao's blog;
  • "I am an AI Safety Researcher", a LessWrong post by Ashe Vazquez Nuñez on the tradeoffs between AI safety and research;
  • Before the game begins, a SPARC JC blog post by Espen Slettnes on why and how our mental constructs of people behave in such non-human-like ways, and why and how those people behave in such non-construct-like ways;
  • i was half myself without you, now i feel so complete, a SPARC JC blog post by Laura Gao on her experience with The Knowledge Society;
  • Supplementing Summer Program for Applied Rationality and Cognition 2026 (SPARC), co-written with David Yu, requesting that Manifund support SPARC; it garnered us $15k in funding.

I am willing to help with writing for purposes other than that of applying to college. Please reach out if you're interested in my editing skills; I'm especially happy to support people writing about music, AI, or their lives.
